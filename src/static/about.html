THE APPROACH / 02

Good software starts
with good questions.

Eskelsen Systems approaches engineering as a craft: understand the business problem, choose the simplest useful design, then verify what was built.

PRINCIPLE 01

Understand first

Define the problem, constraints, and acceptance criteria before making implementation commitments.

PRINCIPLE 02

Build deliberately

Favor maintainable architecture, safe defaults, and clear documentation over unnecessary features.

PRINCIPLE 03

Verify the work

Use targeted tests and honest evidence. A demo is a demo—not a production success story.
